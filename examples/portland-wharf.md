## Summary

Apex Marine Consultants was commissioned to engineer a fast-tracked wharf extension for a bulk material handling facility, enabling the simultaneous mooring and loading of heavy industrial supply barges alongside existing operations.

## Design Requirements

- Support heavy track-mounted mobile cranes (up to 200-tonne operating weight) on the wharf deck.
- Interface with bulk material barges (90m in length and up to 5,000 deadweight tonnage).
- Maintain structural integrity under severe cyclone conditions (Category 5 wind and wave loading).
- Utilize a pre-cast concrete methodology to minimize over-water formwork and concrete pouring.

## Calculations

- Ultimate and serviceability limit state designs IAW AS-4997 and AS-3600.
- Dynamic load allowance (impact factors) for heavy rolling machinery.
- Spatial analysis of pile groups subjected to combined high-vertical loading and lateral ship impacts.
- Cathodic protection system calculations for a 25-year splash-zone durability rating.
- Scour protection and rock armor sizing around the marine substructure.

## Solution

The resulting infrastructure consists of a high-strength, suspended pre-cast concrete deck supported by a grid of raked, heavy-wall steel tubular piles. The design optimized the placement of pre-cast headstocks and plank units, allowing the contractor to build out top-down from the shore without relying on specialized jack-up rigs. 

The structure features integrated cast-in-situ crane rail recesses, quick-release mooring bollards, and an impressed current cathodic protection (ICCP) system to combat aggressive tropical marine corrosion.
