## Summary

Apex Marine Consultants was retained by the municipal transport authority to upgrade an aging commuter ferry berth to support a new fleet of high-capacity, dual-hulled passenger vessels while minimizing disruptions to daily transit schedules.

## Design Requirements

- Accommodate high-frequency berthing of 35-metre high-speed catamarans (250-passenger capacity).
- Integrate seamless, multi-level disability access (DDA compliant) across a 3.2-metre dynamic tidal and wave wake environment.
- Withstand continuous, high-energy impact cycles from rapid-berthing maneuvers.
- Complete the on-site marine installation within a strict 48-hour weekend harbor shutdown window.

## Calculations

- Kinetic energy absorption parameters for high-performance cone fender systems.
- Finite Element Analysis (FEA) of the internal stiffener geometry for the steel pontoon.
- Mooring line tension and catenary calculations for a 4-point heavy chain anchor array.
- Pedestrian crowd-flow dynamics and live load distribution during peak commuter hours.
- Hydroelastic response of the pontoon under combined wind, tide, and vessel wake interaction.

## Solution

The engineering team delivered a heavy-duty, internal-pile-guided steel pontoon fabricated off-site in a dry dock and towed into position. The system incorporates automated, self-leveling hydraulic ramps that adjust dynamically to the ferry deck height, paired with ultra-high-molecular-weight (UHMW) polyethylene rubbing strips and parallel-motion fenders to absorb high berthing energies. 

The entire unit was floated, secured to pre-driven piles, and commissioned within 36 hours of arrival on-site.
