## Summary

Apex Marine Consultants was engaged to design a low-impact, modular guest arrival jetty for a remote eco-resort in Palau. The structure needed to span a sensitive coral reef flat without disrupting the local marine ecosystem or requiring heavy machinery for installation.

## Design Requirements

- Span a 45-metre shallow reef flat with zero footprint on the living coral substrate.
- Support shallow-draft electric catamaran transfers (12m length, 8 tonne displacement).
- Ensure a 50-year design life using non-corrosive, UV-stabilized materials that do not leach chemicals into the marine sanctuary.
- Component weights restricted to under 150kg to allow manual assembly by a local workforce without crane barges.

## Calculations

- Wave attenuation and transmission coefficients across the reef crest.
- Helical screw pile axial and lateral capacities in weak carbonate sand.
- Structural analysis of fiber-reinforced polymer (FRP) sub-frame under cyclic wave loading.
- Buoyancy and stability calculations for the articulating terminal pontoon.
- Hydrodynamic drag forces during extreme storm surge events.

## Solution

The finalized design utilized a modular, lightweight FRP walkway supported by hand-installed helical screw piles driven into isolated sand pockets. The terminal landing features a rotomolded HDPE floating pontoon connected via a dual-axis articulating gangway, accommodating tidal fluctuations while maintaining a stable platform for passenger egress. 

Apex Marine Consultants provided a serialized, step-by-step assembly manual enabling flawless local construction using basic hand tools.
